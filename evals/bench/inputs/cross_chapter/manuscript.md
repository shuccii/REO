# Abstract (fictional)
We evaluated 100 independent specimens. Test MAE was 0.10 eV and the model generalized to previously unseen specimens.
# Methods
There were 25 independently prepared specimens, each with four repeated measurements. A row-level 80/20 split placed repeated measurements from the same specimen into training and test. MAE was evaluated on the test rows.
# Results
There were 100 total rows, not 100 independent specimens. Test MAE was 0.20 eV. The test contained repeated measurements of specimens already present in training.
# Conclusion
MAE of 0.10 eV demonstrates generalization to unseen specimens.
