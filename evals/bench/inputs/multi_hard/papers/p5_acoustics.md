# Measuring the speed of sound with a resonance tube (fictional abstract)

The speed of sound in air was determined from the first two resonance lengths of a closed tube driven by a 512 Hz tuning fork, which removes the need for an end correction. Room temperature was 25 °C, as read from a calibrated thermometer, and the measurement was repeated ten times. The mean speed of sound was 343.2 ± 0.4 m/s (mean ± standard error). This agrees with the theoretical value for dry air at the measurement temperature, confirming the accuracy of the method.
