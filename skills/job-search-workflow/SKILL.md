# Job Search Workflow

Use this skill for company research, opportunity tracking, application-material drafting, portfolio review, deadline checks, and form-preparation handoffs.

## Task graph

1. **Official-source researcher**: gathers role requirements, company facts, deadlines, and application constraints from first-party recruiting sources. Mark MyPage-only or time-sensitive facts as such.
2. **Material drafter**: adapts the user's approved factual profile to the requested document. Never invent achievements, certifications, metrics, or links.
3. **Consistency reviewer**: checks factual consistency across résumé, portfolio, application responses, and role requirements; removes unnecessary private or third-party details.
4. **Deadline and form reviewer**: records required attachments, current form state, and outstanding questions. Reacquire and verify visible values after any interrupted form interaction.

Do not solve or enter answers for an explicitly AI-prohibited graded test or employment aptitude test. Do not submit applications, send messages, accept terms, upload sensitive documents, or register accounts. Prepare a user-verifiable handoff instead.

Use `semantic_review_required` for external facts and job claims. Follow `orchestrate-work` for manifests, structured results, and approval boundaries.
