# Institution template (他大学向けの雛形)

概要(日本語): NAIST 以外の大学で審査や発表の準備をするときに使う確認シートです。公式資料で確かめた事項と、確認できていない事項を分けて書きます。

Record each item as **confirmed (source: …)** or **unconfirmed**. Do not fill gaps with guesses about criteria, scores or pass rates.

## Schedule and format
- Submission deadline for the thesis and abstract:
- Defense date, slot length (presentation and Q&A minutes):
- Language (Japanese / English) and slide or abstract template:
- Who sits on the committee, and whether members outside the subfield attend:

## Criteria
- Official evaluation criteria (list, version and date of the source):
- Whether a scoring rubric exists and whether it is public:
- Required milestones before the defense (for example, progress reports, qualifying exams):

## Local practice
- Advisor or lab sign-off required before submission or presentation:
- Rehearsal required (lab meeting, practice talk):
- Conference or publication requirements tied to the degree:

## Use in this plugin
- Use the confirmed items to build the schedule and the issue log.
- Keep unconfirmed items as questions for the advisor or the program office.
- Never state a pass prediction or a committee's likely questions as fact.
