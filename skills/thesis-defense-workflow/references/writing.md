# Track W: thesis writing checks

By default the student writes the thesis and the agent checks the draft and reports issues; it does not produce text to be submitted as the student's own. Drafting by the agent is possible only in [AI drafting mode](ai-drafting.md), with the advisor's agreement and the student's rewrite of every paragraph.

## Before drafting

1. **Question ↔ answer.** The introduction poses the question; the conclusion answers that same question. Check that they form a closed loop and that the objective's verb can be satisfied by the results.
2. **Outline first.** Write chapter and section headings and the figure list before prose. Each section has one purpose that serves the claim.
3. **Select, do not pour in.** Include the results needed to support the claim; move preliminary or side results to an appendix or drop them. Discuss with the advisor which story the thesis tells.
4. **Cover the comparisons.** For conditions, controls and alternatives, build a small tree (what are the parts / why did this happen / how can it be addressed) at equal granularity, and check that nothing needed is missing and nothing overlaps. A missing control is a likely examiner question.

## While drafting

| Check | Rule |
|---|---|
| Conclusion first | Each chapter and paragraph opens with its point, then reasons and data, then the point again |
| Fact vs interpretation | Separate the observation, the criterion used to judge it, and the judgement |
| One sentence, one idea | Split sentences that carry two claims; keep subject and verb close |
| No double meaning | Rewrite sentences that can be read two ways |
| Terms | Define field terms at first use for a committee member from another subfield; use one term per concept |
| Claim strength | Verbs such as developed, established, proved, clarified match what was actually shown |
| Sources | Primary, citable sources; no reliance on encyclopedias or blogs; every reused figure or text is cited and permitted |
| Figures | Axes, units, conditions and data provenance stated; each figure is referenced in the text |

## Self-review before sending to the advisor

Read the abstract, introduction's last paragraph and conclusion together: they should state the same question, approach, result and contribution. Run research-review-workflow on the draft and log its findings. Then send to the advisor with a short note of what changed and what feedback is wanted.
